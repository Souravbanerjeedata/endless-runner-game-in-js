ENDLESS RUNNER

Choose your world

Pick a destination and get ready to run.

How to Play

Goal

Roll into enemies to score. Reach 150 points for victory; the run also ends if your score goes below 0 or you lose all 5 lives.

Controls

← → Move left / right

↑ Jump

↓ Sit / Dive (in air)

Hold Enter to roll

Rules

Roll or dive into enemies: +1 score.

Hit while not rolling: −1 score and lose a life.

Difficulty increases the longer you play. No time limit.
