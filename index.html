City & Forest Runner

Goal

Reach the score target before time runs out. Level 1: 40 points. Level 2: 100 points.

Controls

← → Move left / right

↑ Jump

↓ Sit / Dive (in air)

Enter Roll (destroy enemies for +1)

Controls

Hold left / right side of screen to move

Swipe up to jump

Swipe down to sit / dive

Double-tap to roll · Double-tap again to stop rolling

Rules

Roll or dive into enemies to score. Touching them without rolling costs 5 score and 1 life. You have 5 lives.
